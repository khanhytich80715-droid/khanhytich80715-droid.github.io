Privacy Policy — AutoAI Memory Desktop

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Who uses this app

AutoAI Memory Desktop is a private desktop tool. It is used only by its operator to manage the operator's own YouTube channels. It has no other users.

Data the app accesses

With the operator's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses these Google scopes:

  • youtube — to upload videos, schedule them, and edit titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and playlists on the operator's own channels;
  • yt-analytics.readonly — to read the operator's own channel reports (impressions, click-through rate, traffic sources).

How data is stored

Access tokens and downloaded reports are stored only on the operator's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's APIs.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone. No data is used for advertising.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token files also removes the app's access.
