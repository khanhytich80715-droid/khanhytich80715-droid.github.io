Privacy Policy — AutoAI Memory Desktop

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Who uses this app

AutoAI Memory Desktop is a private desktop tool. Only its operator uses it. The operator uses it to manage their own YouTube channels and to research video topics on public YouTube data. It has no other users and no public interface.

Data the app accesses

With the operator's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses these Google scopes:

  • youtube — to upload videos, schedule them, and edit titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and playlists on the operator's own channels;
  • yt-analytics.readonly — to read the operator's own channel reports (impressions, click-through rate, traffic sources);
  • youtube.force-ssl (separate token) — only to read public comments. The app never posts, edits or deletes comments.

Public YouTube data (topic research)

The app also reads public YouTube data that does not belong to the operator: search results, public video and channel statistics (such as view and subscriber counts), public video thumbnails and public comments. The operator uses this data only to decide which video topics to make.

From this data the app computes its own metrics, for example the share of videos on a topic whose views are much higher than their channel's subscriber count, and its own topic labels. These are the app's own calculations. They are labelled as such and never presented as YouTube data.

The app does not build profiles of individual viewers. Comment analysis counts topic requests only.

How data is stored and deleted

  • All data is stored only on the operator's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's APIs.
  • Public data that does not belong to the operator (titles, channel names, descriptions, comment text, thumbnails, statistics) is deleted no later than 30 days after it was retrieved, unless it has been refreshed from the API.
  • If the app is accepted for longer storage under the YouTube API Services policies on derived metrics, numeric statistics and the app's own metrics may be kept for up to the period those policies allow (currently 36 months). Text and thumbnails stay on the 30-day rule.
  • Reports about the operator's own channels are kept for as long as the operator needs them, and deleted when access is revoked.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared, published or transferred to anyone. No data is used for advertising.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and to the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and Developer Policies. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token files also removes the app's access. After access is revoked, data obtained with it is deleted within 30 days.
